◆ THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

Hi, I'm Alex.

I make small, strange, colorful things for screens and paper. By day I help a neighborhood record shop make flyers. By night I teach myself HTML, scan everything I own, and rearrange this site for the hundredth time.

I live above a bakery, keep a stack of graph paper beside my keyboard, and think the Internet feels like a giant city where everyone gets to build their own room.
