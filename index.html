WELCOME TO MY HOMEPAGE

Hello, I'm Alex.

I'm 22. I make flyers for a record shop, take pictures when I remember to bring the camera, and spend too much time changing this page. I live with two cats, Miso and Pixel.

There are some things I made here, a journal, and photographs from around the flat. Please have a look around and sign the guestbook before you go.

New photographs are up. I also moved the journal into its own pages. The modem survived the upload.

ABOUT ME

Alex Vale

I live above a bakery and work part time at the record shop down the street. Most of the things on this page were made after work, with scissors, a scanner, and the computer in the spare room.

Jules gave me my first lesson in HTML. I copied the example page into a text editor and changed the words until it felt like mine. Now I keep a notebook of ideas for pages I want to make.

THINGS I MADE

Projects & paper

Record shop flyers

Gig announcements cut from photocopies, hand lettering and whatever paper is left behind the counter. One is still on the telephone pole outside.

The after-hours zine

Eight stapled pages about music, photographs and interesting little corners of the web. The latest issue is on the counter at the shop.

This homepage

My ongoing experiment. I change colors, scan drawings, fix links, and sometimes break them again. If something looks wrong, please tell me.

There are pictures of the work in the photo album.

LINKS AND LISTS

Things I keep nearby

  • At the record shop: a crate of records that nobody has sorted yet.

21 NOV 1998

New page, new cables

I moved the computer again because the desk was wobbling. The modem cable only reaches the wall if I put the table under the window, which means the afternoon sun lands right on the screen. I have taped a piece of cardboard to the window for now.

This is the third version of my homepage. I have finally made a proper page for the photographs, even though the scanner turns every picture a little green. If you find a broken link, please write in the guestbook. I am still learning how to keep all these files in the right folders.

14 NOV 1998

The tape Jules left at my door

Jules left a cassette in my mailbox with a folded track list inside. The handwriting is so small I had to hold it against the kitchen light. I listened to side A twice while cutting letters out for the shop window.

I used to make tapes by sitting beside the radio and waiting for the announcer to stop talking. I still have a few with the first seconds of a song missing. Maybe that is why I like the little mistakes in handmade things. I am putting the tape on again while I finish this page.

03 NOV 1998

Pixel versus the printer

Pixel sat on the paper tray while I was printing flyers. Every sheet came out with a paw print or a stripe of fur, and the printer jammed six times. Miso watched the whole thing from the chair.

The record shop needed forty copies by lunch, so I made the last batch at the copy place on the corner. The black is much better there anyway. I kept one of the bad prints for my wall. The paw print ended up in exactly the right place.

22 OCT 1998

A walk after closing

After the shop closed I took the long way home with the little camera in my coat pocket. The street looked orange under the lamps and blue where the sky was still showing. I took four pictures without thinking much about them.

Three came back too dark. One has a stranger walking through the frame, and I like that one best. I scanned it tonight and put it in the photo album. The scan is muddy, but it feels close to how the walk actually felt.

10 OCT 1998

The day the scanner arrived

My uncle gave me his old flatbed scanner. It weighs more than it looks, and the lid does not close properly over a thick book. I spent the afternoon scanning ticket stubs, sketches and the label from a jar of jam.

Everything has dust on it, even after I wipe the glass. I may leave it there. I like seeing the edge of the paper and the marks from the photocopier. I can finally put things I made by hand onto this site.

28 SEP 1998

A room on the web

When I first saw a personal homepage, I followed the links until I had no idea where I started. Someone had a page about their bicycle, then a page for their cat, then a list of songs they played on rainy days.

It felt more like visiting somebody than reading a magazine. I want this place to feel like that. There is no grand plan. It is a desk with the drawer left open, and you are welcome to look around.

13 SEP 1998

Three hours at the copy shop

The new zine is finally folded and stapled. I took it to the copy shop after breakfast and watched the machine chew through the first stack. The owner let me use the long table by the window to put the pages in order.

A few covers are crooked. I am selling them at the record shop for the price of copying, and I mailed one to my cousin. There is a photograph of the river inside and a list of little websites I keep going back to.

30 AUG 1998

Miso found the warm spot

Miso has decided that the computer is the warmest place in the flat. The first time I turned it on with him asleep on top, he opened one eye and did not move.

Now I have to negotiate every time I want to reach the power switch. I took a picture because nobody believed me. It is in the cats album, along with one of Pixel hiding inside the box of spare cables. They are better at finding things than I am.

18 AUG 1998

My first proper gig flyer

The record shop asked me to make a flyer for Friday night. I drew the letters with a ruler, cut out a photograph from an old magazine, and ran the whole thing through the copier until the edges went dark.

They put one in the window. On Saturday I saw another taped to a telephone pole two streets away. I stood there for a while just looking at it. I never thought a little piece of paper could make the city feel smaller.

29 JUL 1998

Waiting for a photograph

I dropped off a roll of film and had to wait three days to see whether the pictures came out. Half of them are blurred, and on one my thumb covers the corner.

The good ones are in a shoebox under the bed. I used to think I should only keep the sharp pictures, but the blurry ones tell me what was happening around the edges. I will scan a few when I can borrow a scanner.

11 JUL 1998

The page after midnight

I stayed up late changing the background color from blue to yellow and back again. The blue won. The site looked different on the computer at the shop, so I wrote the color numbers on a scrap of paper and tried again at home.

It takes a while to send each new version. I make tea while the files go up. Then I visit the page and find one letter out of place. There is always one more thing to fix.

Hello from the spare room

This is the first note on my new homepage. My computer lives on a table in the spare room, between a stack of records and a plant that I keep forgetting to water.

I am making pages for the things I draw, the places I photograph and the music I keep lending to friends. I do not know who will find this, but if you do, please leave your name in the guestbook. It would be nice to know who came by.
